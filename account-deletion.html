Account & Data Deletion — BursaSearch

If you would like your BursaSearch account and all associated data deleted, you can request this at any time.

How to request deletion

Email us at findmyfunduk@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", sent from the email address linked to your account.

What will be deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile information and any answers you provided in the app
  • Your bursary matches and tracking data

Timeframe

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Once completed, your data cannot be recovered.

Data retention

We do not retain any personal data after your deletion request is completed, except where we are required to keep records by law.
