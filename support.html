Common questions

I didn't get matched to any bursaries — what now?
Open the app and tap "Retake the quiz" from your results page. Answering a few extra questions (especially the optional circumstances ones) often surfaces more matches, since some funds are only for specific situations.

I paid but can't see my full results.
Force-close and reopen the app. If that doesn't fix it, email us with the address linked to your account and roughly when you paid, and we'll sort it out.

How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: Dashboard → Delete Account. This is instant and permanent. Full details on our Account & Data Deletion page.

How do you handle my data?
See our Privacy Policy, linked from the app's Dashboard.

I want a refund.
Email us and we'll take a look — for App Store purchases, refunds are ultimately handled by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com.
